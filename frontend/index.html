        How estimates are calculated

        The estimate comes from fixed rules, not from an AI model. An AI model is used only to read policy text you paste in, and only values it can back with your own words are kept.

        1. Exclusions and waiting periods. If the condition is excluded, or the waiting period has not passed, nothing is payable.
        2. Non-payable items you marked are removed.
        3. Room rent cap. If the daily room rent is above the cap, the room is limited to the cap and related charges (doctor fees, surgery, OT, nursing, tests, procedures) are reduced in the same proportion.
        4. Sub-limits cap individual categories.
        5. Co-pay is taken off what remains.
        6. Deductible is taken off next.
        7. Sum insured is the final ceiling.

        Policies differ. Some apply co-pay before sub-limits, or scale different charges under a room rent cap. If your policy says otherwise, the estimate can be off, which is why every deduction shows the clause it relied on.

        Privacy and data handling

        • Estimates and bills. The numbers you enter are sent to our server to run the calculation. The server does not save them or write them to logs.
        • Policy text. If you use "Read terms", the text is sent to our server and on to an AI provider (Groq) to find the terms. Groq's own terms govern what it does with the text. Leave names, policy numbers and ID numbers out of what you paste.
        • Uploaded documents. The text inside a PDF is read in your browser. For photos and scanned pages, the page image is sent to our server and on to Groq to read the words. For a bill, the text is then sent the same way to find the line items. Nothing is saved on our side. Do not upload documents that belong to someone who has not agreed.
        • Your browser. Nothing is kept after you close or reload the page.
        • Other people. Only enter details about another person, such as a family member, if you have their permission.

        This is an early version. The legal review of how health and financial documents may be handled is still open.
